# 30/60/90 Day Migration Plan

A milestone plan you can hand to your sponsor. It is deliberately conservative — the goal is a plan your team hits, not one that requires perfect execution.

The plan assumes your PoC workload is live in production before day 1. If it is not, your day 1–30 milestone is to get the PoC workload live, then follow this plan from there.

## Day 0: the PoC readout

You leave the PoC sprint with:

- One workload live in production on Lakebase, validated and monitored.
- Measured cost savings (actual, not projected).
- A migration runbook your team owns.
- A proposed Phase 2 scope (next 3–5 workloads, prioritized).
- An identified migration pod lead.

The first 24 hours are about confirming the pod structure and scheduling the Phase 2 kickoff. Do not let momentum lapse between the PoC and Phase 2.

## Day 1–30: stabilize and expand

**Goal:** Phase 1 workload is stable in production. Two additional workloads are migrated and live. Migration pod is operating on its own cadence.

The work in this window is concentrated in three areas:

**Stabilization of the PoC workload:**
- Monitor P95 latency daily; alert if it exceeds the SLA target
- Resolve any edge-case query failures surfaced in production
- Confirm cost savings align with projections (report to executive sponsor)
- Decommission the source platform workload once 30 days are stable

**Migration of Wave 1 workloads (2–3 additional workloads):**
- Choose from the Priority 1 backlog, same workload type as the PoC (easiest learning curve)
- Run the Trust Foundations checklist for each
- Execute the migration sprint; validate against source; cut over
- Re-run the assessor on any newly discovered workloads

**Pod operations setup:**
- Daily standup (15 min): blockers, progress, next steps
- Weekly review with sponsor: progress against plan, savings to date
- Assessment re-run scheduled (monthly)

Typical breakdown of migration pod time:

| Activity | Share of pod time |
| --- | --- |
| Migrating Wave 1 workloads | 50% |
| Monitoring and stabilizing PoC workload | 20% |
| Trust Foundations (SQL compat, access review) for Wave 2 prep | 20% |
| Reporting and coordination | 10% |

**Day 30 milestone:**
- PoC workload decommissioned on source platform (or in parallel with monitoring)
- 2 additional workloads live in production
- Measured savings report delivered to executive sponsor
- Wave 2 scope finalized and sprint planned

If you are not at the milestone at day 30, do not advance. Spend the first week of day 31–60 closing the gap. Overcommitting in Phase 2 while Phase 1 is unstable creates incidents.

---

## Day 31–60: drive to meaningful scale

**Goal:** 5–8 total workloads migrated and stable. At least one domain fully migrated. Executive sponsor can see the savings line moving on the dashboard.

This window shifts toward breadth — migrating the remaining Priority 1 workloads — and begins the Evaluate workloads that require prep.

**Migration of Wave 2 (Priority 1 remainder):**
- Complete all remaining Priority 1 workloads
- If any have blockers, those blockers should have been resolved in days 1–30
- Aim to have all Priority 1 workloads in production by day 60

**Begin Evaluate workloads:**
- Pick 1–2 Evaluate workloads with the lowest complexity scores
- Run the pre-migration refactoring sprint (UDF rewrites, schema simplification)
- Begin migration — these take longer than Priority 1

**Domain completion:**
- If a domain has all its workloads at Priority 1, complete it fully by day 60
- A fully migrated domain enables decommissioning a schema or account on the source platform — that is where the largest savings materialize

**Day 60 milestone:**
- All Priority 1 workloads live in production
- At least one domain fully migrated (no source-platform dependency remaining)
- Savings tracking dashboard visible to executive sponsor
- 1–2 Evaluate workloads in active migration sprint

This is the milestone you can show externally. Most teams schedule a "migration program review" at the day-60 mark; it is the moment the program crosses from proof to progress.

---

## Day 61–90: accelerate and hand off

**Goal:** Evaluate workloads in production. Migration pod operating independently. Phase 3 (remaining estate or optimization) scoped and committed.

**Evaluate workload completion:**
- Complete migrations started in days 31–60
- Run the full SQL compatibility check on remaining Evaluate workloads
- Start migration sprints for next wave

**Source platform decommission planning:**
- For each decommissioned domain, initiate the formal source platform retirement process
- Most platforms have contract renewal windows — hit these to realize the savings
- Document the decommission plan for each source platform in scope

**Optimization:**
- Review Lakebase compute usage across all migrated workloads; right-size warehouse configuration
- Enable Lakebase caching for any workload with repeated identical queries
- Review cost actuals vs. projections; adjust savings estimates for executive reporting

**Phase 3 scoping:**
- Evaluate the Hold workloads: have any become Priority 1 due to increased pain or lower complexity?
- Scope the remaining estate migration (Heavy ETL refactoring, complex Evaluate workloads)
- Propose Phase 3 timeline and resource commitment to executive sponsor

**Day 90 milestone:**
- All Evaluate workloads targeted in Phase 2 are live in production
- At least one source platform decommission is initiated
- Phase 3 scope and budget is approved
- Migration pod has a clear 90-day forward plan

## What is not in this plan

- **Migrating Hold workloads.** These require significant prep (refactoring, redesign). Hold them in the backlog for Phase 3.
- **Standing up new pipelines.** Existing pipelines are updated to point at Lakebase; no new infrastructure is built during this period.
- **Full organizational training.** Business user training on Lakebase AI/BI features is a Phase 3 program; the first 90 days focus on technical migration.

## Adjusting the plan

The shape of this plan is durable. The numbers are not:

- Fewer than 10 workloads total: compress to a 15/30/60 plan
- More than 50 workloads: extend to 45/90/135 and consider a paid co-delivery for Phase 2
- High Evaluate ratio (> 60%): extend days 1–30 to 45 days to allow refactoring sprints to complete

The savings dashboard is the source of truth. If the line is not moving on the expected cadence, the plan needs adjusting — not the team.
