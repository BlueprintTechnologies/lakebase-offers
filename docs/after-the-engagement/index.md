# After the Engagement

The readiness assessment and PoC are the beginning of your Lakebase program, not the end. This section is the operational guide for what comes next: standing up the migration team, executing in phases, measuring success, and scaling to your full estate.

## The four phases after a successful PoC

| Phase | Timeline | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[30/60/90 Day Plan](30-60-90.md)** | Starts immediately after PoC | First workload in production; team trained; Phase 2 committed |
| **[Standing Up Your Migration Pod](migration-pod.md)** | Week 1 | Dedicated team formed; cadence established; tools set up |
| **[Measuring Migration Success](measuring-success.md)** | Ongoing | Validate savings, performance, and reliability in production |
| **[Scaling to More Workloads](second-workload.md)** | After first workload is stable | Apply patterns from Phase 1 to Phase 2 workloads |

## What you walk out of the PoC with

A successful Blueprint PoC sprint delivers:

1. **One working production migration.** A Priority 1 workload running in production on Lakebase, validated against the source platform, with 72 hours of monitoring complete.
2. **Validated cost savings.** Actual Lakebase compute cost for 2 weeks of the migrated workload, comparable to source platform cost.
3. **A migration runbook.** The step-by-step playbook your team used to migrate the PoC workload — reusable for Phase 2.
4. **A Phase 2 scope.** The next 3–5 workloads, prioritized by Opportunity Score, with effort estimates and a proposed timeline.
5. **Team knowledge transfer.** Your tech lead has executed the migration end-to-end and can lead Phase 2 independently.
